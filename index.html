Unit 1 Grammar: Our School Supplies & Objects

Welcome to Unit 1! Based on National Geographic's Our World 1, we will explore how to ask and answer questions about classroom objects using Yes/No questions with it's, What, and How many.

Grammar I

Yes / No Questions with *it's*

We use Is it a / an...? to ask if an object is something specific.

Question Answer (Affirmative) Answer (Negative)
Is it a pen? Yes, it is. It's a pen. No, it isn't. It's a crayon.
Is it an eraser? Yes, it is. It's an eraser. No, it isn't. It's a pencil.

💡 Rule: Use an before words starting with vowels (a, e, i, o, u) like eraser, and a before consonants like pen, table, desk.

Grammar II

What and How Many

We use What to ask about identity or color, and How many to ask about quantity.

What is it? $\rightarrow$ It's a computer. / It's blue.
How many erasers? $\rightarrow$ Three. / Five books.

🏫 Vocabulary Bank: pen, pencil, eraser, ruler, book, notebook, backpack, desk, chair, table, board, crayon, computer, map, clock.

Advanced Level Examples (11 - 15) - Multi-Rule Integration
Example 11

Complex Article Selection with Modified Nouns

Prompt: Choose 'a' or 'an' for __ eraser vs __ green pen.

Step-by-Step Solution:

1. For 'eraser', initial letter is 'e' (vowel) $\rightarrow$ an eraser.
2. For 'pen', initial letter is 'p' (consonant) $\rightarrow$ a pen.
3. Conclusion: Rule depends strictly on the immediate noun/adjective following the article.
Example 12

Multi-Step Dialogue Correction

Error Fix: "Is it a eraser? No, it is not. It's a book."

Step-by-Step Solution:

1. Check article before vowel: change 'a eraser' to an eraser.
2. Check negative contraction: change 'is not' to isn't.
3. Corrected dialogue: "Is it an eraser? No, it isn't. It's a book."
Example 13

Synthesizing Quantity and Color

Prompt: Write question and answer for 4 green books.

Step-by-Step Solution:

1. Quantity question: How many green books?
2. Answer: Four.
3. Identity/Color question: What color are they? They're green.
Example 14

Classroom Object Riddle Analysis

Riddle: "It is white. It is on the wall. We write on it with markers. What is it?"

Step-by-Step Solution:

1. Analyze clues: white, on wall, marker board.
2. Match vocabulary: whiteboard.
3. Answer: It's a whiteboard.
Example 15

Complete Peer Interview Script Creation

Task: Create a 3-turn dialogue between two students asking about school items.

Step-by-Step Solution:

Student A: "Is it a map?"
Student B: "No, it isn't. It's a computer."
Student A: "What color is it?"
Student B: "It's gray!"

Critical Reflection & Thinking

Open questions designed for classroom discussion, logical analysis, and teacher-led inquiry.

Question 1

Why do we use 'an' instead of 'a' before words like 'eraser'?

Think about how words sound when you speak. Saying "a eraser" sounds bumpy and difficult to say smoothly. Adding 'n' ("an eraser") makes speech flow easily between vowels! Can you think of other classroom words that need 'an'?

Question 2

How does asking 'How many' help us organize our classroom?

Counting supplies like books, crayons, and pencils helps us share materials responsibly with our classmates and keep our Colegio Montano desks clean and orderly.
